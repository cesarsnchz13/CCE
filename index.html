Our Approach

Rooted in reflective practice

Chaplaincy Clinical Education, LLC (CCE) is an online/hybrid training center led by Rev. Carlos A. Sánchez, ACPE Certified Educator. We specialize in adult‑learning pedagogy and culturally responsive formation for chaplains across healthcare, hospice, congregations, and community contexts.

  • Story‑based reflective practice
  • Bowen Family Systems & Enneagram
  • Research‑informed spiritual care
  • Interdisciplinary collaboration

Rev. Carlos A. Sánchez

ACPE Certified Educator • Founder

Rev. Sánchez dedicates his work to forming compassionate, reflective, and culturally responsive spiritual caregivers. Ordained by the American Baptist Churches, USA, he brings over a decade of pastoral experience serving diverse communities.

As a bilingual educator, he facilitates CPE programs in English and Spanish, integrating theological reflection, psychological theory, and spiritual care practice. His teaching emphasizes personal transformation, intercultural competence, and professional integrity grounded in faith and compassion.

Program Pathways

Choose from Extended, Intensive, and Residency‑track learning experiences meeting the ACPE 400‑hour standard.

Level 1A – Foundations

Core skills in presence, assessment frameworks (FICA/HOPE), charting, and spiritual care plans.

  • Verbatims & IPR
  • Theology of Story
  • Ethics & Self‑care

Level 1B – Integration

Broaden competencies with family systems, cultural humility, and interdisciplinary care.

  • Bowen Theory
  • Cultural Responsiveness
  • Research Literacy

  • Quality Improvement

  • Portfolio Coaching

Frequently Asked Questions

Is CCE an ACPE‑accredited center?

Yes, Chaplaincy Clinical Education is fully accredited by ACPE. Our program meets national standards, and units earned here are recognized by major chaplaincy certification bodies (APC, NACC, NAJC).

Do you offer bilingual (EN/ES) cohorts?

Yes. We offer Spanish‑forward cohorts where verbatims and readings are in Spanish, as well as bilingual supervision for mixed cohorts.

What are the requirements for online CPE?

You need a stable internet connection, a webcam for Zoom sessions, and a private space to ensure confidentiality during group process. You also need a clinical placement site (we can help you negotiate this if you don't have one).
